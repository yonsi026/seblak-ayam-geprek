Your task is to design and build a premium, modern, clean, highly-converting landing page for a local Indonesian food business called:

“Seblak Prasmanan dan Ayam Geprek Sereh”

The landing page must feel like a combination of:

Swiss International Design + Modern Indonesian Food Brand + Digital Food Counter + High-Converting E-Commerce

Seblak bisa diracik sesuai selera. Ayam geprek punya cita rasa khas. Pesan dengan cepat.

The primary goal is CONVERSION — turning visitors into food orders.
